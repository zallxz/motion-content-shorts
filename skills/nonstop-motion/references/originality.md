# Originality guardrails

The skill is inspired by principles, never by copying. Follow these rules for every video:

1. **No copyrighted characters, logos or mascots** (Disney, anime, game characters, other creators' mascots). If asked, offer an original mascot instead (`extending.md`). A brand's *own* logo or mascot is fine when the creator owns it.
2. **Don't clone a creator's signature format.** Don't reuse another account's recurring title pattern, catchphrase or series name word for word. Take the mechanic (e.g. "talking to an AI companion") and write it in the creator's own voice.
3. **No fake facts.** Stats must be real and sourced, or clearly marked as examples. Never fabricate quotes, screenshots of real people's chats, or real company dashboards with invented numbers.
4. **UI mockups are generic.** Use invented app names (`my-app`, `Bank lu`) rather than imitating a real product's exact interface. Naming a real product in text is OK when it's commentary.
5. **Music:** recommend royalty-free or platform-licensed audio only.
6. **Fonts:** bundled fonts are SIL Open Font License (Inter, Space Grotesk, Bricolage Grotesque, JetBrains Mono, Instrument Serif). Added fonts must have a commercial-OK license.
