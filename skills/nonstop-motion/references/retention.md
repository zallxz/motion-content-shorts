# Retention: making scrollers stop and stay

## The first 0.5 seconds
- **Frame 0 shows the hook.** The engine forces a `cut` entrance on beat 1; never add a fade-in or logo intro.
- The hook's words slam in one at a time with a micro-shake. Motion catches the eye before reading starts.
- A `kicker` pill gives context instantly ("POV", "Jumat 16:59", "Myth #1", "Part 2").
- A mascot with a strong emotion (shocked/smug) on beat 1 reads faster than any text.

## Hook formulas (fill the blanks, keep it to 8 words or fewer)
| formula | Indonesian example | English example |
|---|---|---|
| POV + situation | POV: lu nyuruh AI benerin 1 bug | POV: you let AI fix one bug |
| Contradiction | Gaji naik, tabungan malah turun | Raise up, savings down |
| Number shock | Otak lu nilai desain dalam 50 ms | Your brain judges design in 50 ms |
| Wrong belief | Lu salah soal minum kopi | You're wrong about coffee |
| Stakes / time | Jumat 16:59. Deploy dikit doang. | Friday 4:59. Small deploy. |
| Secret / insider | Trik yang gak diajarin di kampus | What school never taught you |
| Direct callout | Kalau lu freelancer, stop scroll | Freelancers, stop scrolling |
| Unfinished action | Gw coba hidup tanpa HP 7 hari… | I tried 7 days without a phone… |

Avoid hooks that give the answer away, vague hype ("This will change your life"), and clickbait the video doesn't pay off.

## Keeping them: pattern interrupts
- **Visual change at least every 1–3 s.** The tempo system guarantees this, and the rotating transitions (push → zoom → whip → drop) keep consecutive cuts from feeling the same.
- **Change beat type at least every 2 beats.** Text → UI → dialog → stat feels like a new scene each time.
- **A `punch` about every 3–4 beats.** The full-screen color flood resets attention.
- **Open loop:** promise something in the hook or beat 2 that is resolved at the end ("yang ke-3 paling penting").
- **Progress bar** (on by default): segmented, so viewers see that it's short and how much is left. This reduces drop-off.

## Ending: CTA + loop
Match the CTA to the goal:
- **Follow:** series content, "Follow buat part 2"
- **Save:** lists and tips, "Save biar gak lupa"
- **Share:** relatable content, "Kirim ke temen yang…"
- **Comment:** an opinion or question, "Tim A atau B? Komen"

**Loop trick:** write the last line so it flows into the first. If the hook is "Lu nyuruh AI benerin 1 bug", end with a CTA like "…dan besoknya lu nyuruh lagi." Replays count heavily in Reels and Shorts ranking.

## Comprehension at speed (fast but clear)
1. **One idea per beat.** Speed comes from cutting more often, not from cramming text into each beat.
2. **Emphasis guides the eye.** One accent word per beat, so the viewer reads it first.
3. **Show the thing.** Use UI, chat, stat and compare beats instead of describing.
4. **Consistent screen positions.** Text sits in the center band and the mascot in the lower third, so the eye doesn't hunt.
5. **Computed timing.** Each beat lasts until it can be read, then cuts. Nothing more.
6. **Sound-off first.** Every word that matters is on screen; audio is a bonus.
